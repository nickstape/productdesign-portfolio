<Section id="tldr" label="tldr" nav="TLDR" lead title="NDD is where Nigeria’s recurring payments get permission.">

NIBSS Direct Debit is the electronic mandate library behind Nigeria’s direct debit scheme. When an insurer, lender or utility collects a recurring payment from a customer’s bank account, the mandate that allows it is created, approved and monitored here. It’s a back-office product: the people who use it do so all day, under audit, with real money on the line.

<AudienceCards>
  <AudienceCard group="Billers and aggregators" title="Raise the mandates" roles="Biller Initiator · Bank as Aggregator">Insurers, lenders and utilities create mandates for their customers, one at a time or hundreds in a bulk upload.</AudienceCard>
  <AudienceCard group="Banks" title="Check and approve them" roles="Initiator · Authoriser · Auditor · Admin · Bank as Aggregator">The payer’s bank captures, approves, rejects and audits each mandate, with duties split between people.</AudienceCard>
  <AudienceCard group="NIBSS" title="Run the scheme" roles="NIBSS Admin">Scheme admins oversee every bank, biller, aggregator, institution and user, plus reporting and billing.</AudienceCard>
</AudienceCards>

<Section id="reflection" label="what I'd do differently" nav="Reflection" title="The lesson I took from NDD: in regulated systems, the permission model is the product, and the screens follow from it.">

<Bullets>

- **Measure from day one.** Agree activation time and approval turnaround as success metrics with the PM before build.
- **Finish the accessibility work.** Roll the audit fixes out to every screen, add visible focus states and test with a screen reader.
- **Design the unhappy paths.** Bulk upload needs a row-level error report; expired or cancelled mandates need their own states.
- Iterate fail state with Claude Design & Figma MCP.

</Bullets>
